    Open one or more DICOM files. Use the frame slider or arrow buttons to browse a series. Drag to pan and pinch to zoom.

    Supports common uncompressed grayscale and RGB DICOM, DICOM RLE, and baseline JPEG. Some files use unsupported formats such as JPEG 2000 or JPEG Lossless.
